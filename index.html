OngoingJuly 2026 – Present

Adaptive Neural-Impedance Control for a Wrist Rehabilitation Robot Incorporating Gamification

  • Developed a control framework for a 3-DOF wrist rehabilitation robot that combines adaptive impedance control, RBF-based neural estimation of biomechanical properties and fatigue, and sensorless interaction-torque observation.
  • Integrated robot dynamics, spasm detection, safety mechanisms, and gamification to enable patient-adaptive, safe rehabilitation, validated through MATLAB simulations under varying interaction and fatigue conditions.
MATLAB · RBF Neural Networks · Adaptive Control · Robotics
